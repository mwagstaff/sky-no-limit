# Design Context — Sky No Limit (skynolimit.dev)

## Audience
Prospective clients looking for an experienced partner to design and build
high-quality mobile and web products. They should feel: "these people are
technically excellent, creatively ambitious, and easy to trust."

## Use cases
- Evaluate the studio's credibility and taste within seconds of landing
- Browse real shipped work (App Store apps) with challenge/result context
- Start a project enquiry (email CTA)

## Brand personality / tone
**"Cinematic orbital studio"** — premium, confident, calm. Space as
atmosphere and a metaphor for ambition, never as neon sci-fi wallpaper.
Copy is concise, specific, professional; no buzzwords, no invented metrics,
no playful filler ("princess powered", "dino-soar" are retired).

## Visual system
- Palette: near-black blue/indigo base (`oklch(15% 0.026 262)` family),
  luminous icy blue (`oklch(85% 0.09 224)`) as the single signal colour,
  restrained comet amber (`oklch(80% 0.125 66)`) for rare moments of energy
  (case numerals, contact headline, focus rings).
- Type: Fraunces (display serif, variable, italics for accent words) +
  Instrument Sans (body). Self-hosted. Never Inter/Roboto/Arial/monospace.
- Signature visual: full-width planet limb with icy rim light across the
  hero's lower band, tilted orbit line with a slowly moving craft, sparse
  canvas starfield, occasional amber meteor. Subtle cursor parallax.
- No glassmorphism, no glow-border cards, no gradient headline text,
  no icon-in-a-card grids, no mobile-style bottom nav on desktop.
- Motion: hero copy reveals first, orbital settles after; scroll reveals;
  everything honours `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Constraints
- Real content only — four real App Store apps, no fabricated logos/metrics.
- WCAG-conscious contrast, visible amber focus states, semantic landmarks.
- Performance: no video backgrounds, tiny JS (<5kB), lazy images,
  self-hosted fonts.
