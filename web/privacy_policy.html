How Sky No Limit handles information across Train Track UK, our other apps and this website.

Sky No Limit is operated by Mike Wagstaff in London, United Kingdom. The details differ by service, so choose the section that applies to you.

TL;DR

Free apps, built for the fun of it.

All Sky No Limit apps are free hobby projects, built and operated because (I think) they're useful, and they're fun to make. They don't make any money. 01 · App-specific notice

Train Track UK

This section applies only to Train Track UK and takes priority if it differs from the general section below. The app does not require an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or phone number.

Information saved on your device

  • Saved journeys: journey legs, origin and destination stations, creation date, favourites and your chosen ordering.
  • Journey history: stations, services, operators, scheduled and observed times, calling points, station arrival and departure events, outcomes, delays and Delay Repay status. The app keeps up to 2,000 of the newest history records.
  • Location: precise location is used on your device to rank nearby stations, detect station arrivals, provide destination alerts and record journey history. The most recent coordinates and timestamp are cached for the app and its widgets. Raw coordinates are not sent to our backend.

Saved journeys and history are not synchronised through an app account or our servers. We do not build iCloud sync into the app, although Apple may include app data in a device backup depending on your settings. If you export history as a CSV file, you choose where it is sent and the chosen destination's privacy terms apply.

Information processed by our backend

The app creates a random installation identifier. It is not your Apple advertising identifier and does not contain your name or contact details, but we treat it as personal data because it can distinguish one app installation from another. We associate it with:

  • App settings and notification preferences.
  • Notification subscriptions, including push tokens, routes and stations, selected days and time windows, notification types and delivery state.
  • Live Activity identifiers, push tokens and journey state while a Live Activity is running.
  • Station-area events used for arrival features, including the installation identifier, time, event identifier, relevant station codes and source IP address. These events do not contain raw coordinates.
  • Request and diagnostic information such as IP address, user agent, requested routes, status codes and errors, used to operate and secure the service.

Why we use it

We rely on our legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) of the UK GDPR: operating Train Track UK, providing features you choose to use, delivering notifications and Live Activities, maintaining security and reliability, preventing misuse and diagnosing faults. We use only the information reasonably necessary for those purposes and have balanced these interests against users' rights and reasonable expectations.

Apple's system permissions control access to location and notifications. You can change them at any time in your device settings without those permissions being used for advertising or unrelated analytics.

Service providers and international transfers

  • Hetzner: our backend and MongoDB database are hosted by Hetzner Online GmbH using its Helsinki, Finland region. Hetzner processes this information under a data processing agreement. Germany and Finland are in the European Economic Area and covered by the UK's adequacy regulations, so no additional transfer mechanism is required for this hosting.
  • Cloudflare: Cloudflare, Inc. carries website and API traffic through its global network, so it may process IP addresses, request metadata and request or response content in transit. Cloudflare's data processing terms use the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework for covered transfers to the United States, with contractual safeguards for other relevant transfers.
  • Apple: optional notifications and Live Activities are delivered through Apple Push Notification service, which receives a device push token and notification payload. Apple may process information internationally under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

Timetable and service information comes from railway data providers. Requests to those providers are made by our backend and do not include your random installation identifier or raw location.

How long we keep it and how to delete it

  • Saved journeys remain on your device until you remove them. Journey history is limited to the newest 2,000 records; you can delete individual records or clear all history in the app.
  • Scheduled notification subscriptions remain until you delete them. Live notification sessions and Live Activities normally expire within two hours.
  • Most delivery, diagnostic, audit and station-area event records are automatically removed after 7 to 30 days.
  • Installation preferences remain while needed to provide the service, or until you ask us to delete the server data associated with your installation.

To request deletion of app-managed server data, copy the Support ID from Train Track UK under About › Support and email it to Mike Wagstaff. Local journeys and history can be removed in the app or by removing its data from your device.

02 · General notice

Other apps and this website

This section applies to the Sky No Limit website and every Sky No Limit app except Train Track UK, unless an app presents a more specific notice.

Information we process

  • We do not use advertising, profiling, unrelated analytics or tracking cookies on this website.
  • If you dismiss the cookie notice, your browser stores that preference for six months so we do not show it again. It is not used for tracking.
  • Our hosting and delivery infrastructure processes standard request information, such as IP addresses and HTTP request details, to deliver and secure the website and app services.
  • Where an app offers saved preferences or notifications, it may use a random installation identifier to associate those choices with that installation.
  • If you contact us by email, we use the details and message you provide to respond.

Why we use it and who receives it

We rely on our legitimate interests in operating, securing, maintaining and improving our services, providing features you request and responding to messages. Information is kept only for as long as reasonably needed for those purposes.

Hetzner hosts our services in Helsinki, Finland. Cloudflare carries website and API traffic through its global network. The location and transfer safeguards described in the Train Track UK section also apply to these providers when they support our other services.

03 · Applies to everyone

Your rights and contact details

Sky No Limit is the controller for the processing described in this policy. It is operated by Mike Wagstaff in London, United Kingdom. Our services are not designed or marketed specifically for children, and we do not use personal data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Your choices and rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict its use, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. To make a request, email Mike Wagstaff. We may need enough information to locate the relevant app installation and verify the request.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office, the UK data protection regulator.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when a service or its data handling changes. We will publish the revised version at this same URL and change the date below. If another app begins processing information that is not covered by the general notice, we will add its own section here.
