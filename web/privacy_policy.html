Privacy Policy

Summary

Sky No Limit operates the skynolimit.dev website and apps.

In summary, for this website and all our apps…

  • We don't store any personally identifiable information unless you provide it to us (e.g. by contacting us)
  • We limit data collation as far as possible (no time to read data when there's football to be played)
  • We don't sell your data (there's enough gold in the kingdom coffers)

What data do we collect?

The only data we collect is technical information…

  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website
  • If you dismiss our cookie notice, your browser stores that preference for six months so we don't show it again. It isn't used for tracking
  • Our hosting infrastructure may process standard website data, such as IP addresses and HTTP request details, to deliver and secure the site
  • For our apps, we store device IDs (unique device identifiers containing no personally identifiable information) in order to record per-user settings and preferences (e.g. notifications)
  • We don't ask for names, email addresses or phone numbers on this website. If you contact us by email, we use the details you provide to respond

What do we do with the data?

What we do with the data…

  • We don't sell or share this information
  • We only use it to review and improve our offerings
