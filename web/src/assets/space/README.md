# Nebula and world textures

NASA imagery, public domain, credited in the footer. Planet maps are
equirectangular (longitude −180° at the left edge, north up), wrapped onto the
worlds by `src/scene/env/system.js`; the nebula is layered by
`src/scene/env/nebula.js`. Loaded after the opening is on screen.

| File | Used for | Source | Processing |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `nebula-veil-2048.webp` | The Capabilities nebula | Hubble, Veil Nebula supernova remnant (NGC 6960), STScI release 2015-29a, 6000×4000 (`stsci_2015-29a_6000.jpg`) from [astropix.org](https://www.astropix.org/image/stsci/2015-29a). Credit: NASA, ESA, and the Hubble Heritage Team (STScI/AURA) | Area-resized to 3072×2048. Stars removed: compact points that no 15 px line opening (12 orientations) keeps, plus round saturated cores that stay islands below 50% brightness, with an X for their diffraction spikes; holes filled by Telea inpainting. Sky background (22nd percentile) taken to black, sulphur greens pulled 70% towards gold, gamma 1.12; Lanczos to 2048×1365; `cwebp -q 82 -sharp_yuv` |
| `jupiter-2048.webp` | Top Scores | Cassini, "Cassini's Best Maps of Jupiter (Cylindrical Map)", PIA07782, 3601×1801, from [NASA Science](https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/cassinis-best-maps-of-jupiter-cylindrical-map/). Credit: NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute | Area-resized to 2048×1024, saturation ×1.25; `cwebp -q 80` |
| `mars-1536.webp` | Bikespot London | Viking MDIM 2.1 colorized global mosaic (1 km), 3840 px rendition of `Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_1km.jpg` via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mars_Viking_MDIM21_ClrMosaic_1km.jpg) from [USGS Astrogeology](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/mars_viking_colorized_global_mosaic_232m). Credit: NASA/JPL/USGS | Area-resized to 2048×1024, Lanczos to 1536×768; `cwebp -q 76` |

Credit: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio | Unchanged; `cwebp -q 72` / `-q 85`. The heights drive bump mapping |

Credit: NASA/JPL/USGS | The mosaic covers 60°N–60°S and longitudes 100–345°, and repeats itself past column 2266 (correlation puts the repeat at 1884 px). A believable Io, not a geographically exact one |
| `europa-1024.webp` | TrainTrack UK | Voyager/Galileo SSI global mosaic, 1024×512 (`Europa_Voyager_GalileoSSI_global_mosaic.jpg`) via [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Europa_Voyager_GalileoSSI_global_mosaic.jpg) from [USGS Astrogeology](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/europa_voyager_galileo_ssi_global_mosaic_500m). Credit: NASA/JPL/USGS | Greyscale mosaic split into fine detail (minus a σ 6 px masked blur) and broad albedo (σ 60 px, ×0.6); detail contrast evened between Galileo and Voyager tiles (gain 0.75–1.6 over σ 40 px), so tile edges and the missing south pole don't show; coloured blue-white ice to rust-brown in the lineae; `cwebp -q 85` |
