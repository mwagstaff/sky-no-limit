TrainTrack UK

iOS · watchOS · Live rail data

UK rail apps tend to bury the one thing you care about — is my train on time, and which platform? — under adverts and ticket upsells.

TrainTrack strips journey-tracking back to the essentials: realtime departures with delay and cancellation warnings, replacement-bus alerts, saved journeys pinned to your home screen, and a companion Apple Watch app. Completely free.

Top Scores

iOS · Push notifications · Prediction engine

Score apps ruin the match you're watching on a stream delay, and never tell you where to watch the next one.

Top Scores follows UK and top international football with a configurable delay on score notifications, UK TV listings folded into kick-off alerts, curated news feeds, and a ratings-driven predictor that simulates fixtures before they're played.

My Boris Bikes

iOS · watchOS · TfL live data

Checking a Santander Cycles dock shouldn't need a login and a loading spinner — you just want bikes, e-bikes and spaces at a glance.

My Boris Bikes focuses on the docks you actually use: live availability from TfL's data feed, no registration, a watch app and widgets — and it's generally far quicker than the official app in day-to-day use.

A small studio covering the whole journey.

Strategy, design, engineering and the infrastructure underneath — the same hands take a product from sketch to something people rely on.

Product strategy
Shaping the idea before a line of code is written: scoping, prototyping, and honest advice about what to build first — and what to leave out. We've made those calls on our own products, at our own risk.
Web experiences
Fast, accessible web apps and the APIs behind them, built and self-hosted end to end — realtime data services, push notification pipelines and production platforms we operate ourselves.
Mobile apps
Native iOS apps with companion watchOS apps and widgets — four currently live on the App Store. Designed for speed, built to feel at home on the platform, and maintained long after launch.
